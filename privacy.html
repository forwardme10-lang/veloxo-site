Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Veloxo is a cycling app for riders and cycling clubs in Chennai, India. It is run by Vasanth Sankar as an individual (“Veloxo”, “we”). This policy explains what personal data the Veloxo app collects, why, who it is shared with, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we only use your data to run Veloxo for you.

1. Data we collect

WhatExamplesWhy

ProfileCity, phone number, date of birth, gender, cycling club, riding style, level, weekly hours, years of ridingTo set up your training and your event registrations

Rides from your deviceFor each ride: start time, duration, distance, average power, average heart rate, climbing. Only after you connect Wahoo or Intervals.icu (which can bring rides from Zwift, Garmin and other services you link there)To track your training and adapt your plan

We don't collect your location, contacts or files. The only photo we store is a profile photo, if you choose to add one.

2. Who can see your data

  • You. Everything above.
  • Clubs you register with. Your name, contact details, chosen distance, bib, payment status and UTR, attendance, results and emergency details, for that event only.
  • Everyone. Published results show your first name and last initial, your time and rank. Club and event details are public.
  • Veloxo. To run and support the app (for example to review clubs or answer a support request).

4. How long we keep data

  • Your account data is kept while your account exists, and deleted when you delete your account (see Delete your account).
  • Device connections are removed as soon as you disconnect them.
  • Database backups are overwritten within 7 days.

5. Your rights

You can:

  • see and correct your data in the app (Profile, Edit profile, Settings);
  • withdraw consent: turn off notifications, disconnect devices, or stop using Velo;
  • ask for a copy of your data or ask us to delete it, by emailing veloxo.si@gmail.com;
  • delete your account at any time;
  • raise a complaint with us (below) and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Children

Veloxo is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, sign-in sessions are stored encrypted on your phone, and database rules limit each person to their own data (and clubs to their own events). Keys for outside services are kept on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Health and fitness data

Heart rate, power, weight and medical notes are used only for your training, your coaching and your safety at events. They are never used for advertising and never sold.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app before important changes apply.

10. Contact and grievance officer

Vasanth Sankar · Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India · veloxo.si@gmail.com. We reply to privacy requests within 30 days.
