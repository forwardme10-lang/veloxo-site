Common questions

I paid for an event but my registration isn't confirmed

Entry fees go straight to the club's UPI ID. The club checks the transaction number (UTR) you entered and confirms it, usually within 24 hours. If it's been longer, contact the club from the event page, or email us.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are paid by the club that runs the event, following the refund policy shown on the event page. Veloxo doesn't receive or hold entry fees.

How do I connect my Wahoo device?

In the app, open your profile → Devices → Wahoo → Connect, and sign in to Wahoo. You can disconnect it the same way at any time.

Can I ride my workouts in Zwift or on a Garmin?

Yes, through Intervals.icu (free). In the app, open your profile → Devices → Intervals.icu → Connect. Then, in Intervals.icu settings, connect Zwift and/or Garmin Connect. Your workouts go there, and your rides come back to Veloxo.

Velo said something wrong or inappropriate

Velo is a Sports Intelligence (SI) coach. It runs on AI and can make mistakes. Please email us the message and the date. Velo's advice is general training guidance, not medical advice.

I want to report an event or an organiser

Email us the event name and what's wrong. We review every report and can unpublish events or remove organisers.
