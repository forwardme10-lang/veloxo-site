01

Who can use Veloxo

You must be 18 or older and give accurate information. Keep your sign-in details safe; you’re responsible for what happens in your account.

02

Training plans and Velo

  • Training plans and Velo’s replies are general fitness guidance. They are not medical advice. Velo, our Sports Intelligence (SI) coach, runs on AI and can be wrong.
  • Check with a doctor before starting or increasing training, especially if you have a medical condition. Stop and get help if you feel unwell.
  • Ride safely and follow traffic rules. You are responsible for your own riding, equipment and safety.
03

Events

  • Events on Veloxo are organised and run by independent cycling clubs, not by Veloxo. The club is responsible for the event, the route, safety arrangements, results and refunds.
  • When you register, you agree to the event’s rules shown on its page (for example helmets, age limits, cut-off times).
  • Cycling events carry a risk of injury. You take part at your own risk, as described in the event’s rules and any waiver the club asks for.
04

Payments

  • Entry fees are paid directly to the club (for example to the club’s UPI ID). Veloxo does not receive, hold or refund entry fees.
  • Refunds and distance changes follow the refund policy on the event page and are paid by the club.
  • Veloxo is free during testing. If we add paid features later, we’ll show the price and terms before you pay.
05

For clubs (organisers)

  • Clubs are reviewed before they can publish events. Give accurate information about your club, events, prices and payment details.
  • You’re responsible for running your events lawfully and safely, including permits, insurance, marshals and refunds.
  • Use riders’ personal data (including emergency and medical details) only to run your event and keep riders safe. Don’t share it or use it for marketing.
  • Confirm payments honestly and promptly. We may unpublish events or remove clubs that mislead riders or break these terms.
06

  • Impersonate others, enter fake transaction numbers, or try to access data that isn’t yours.

Report problems to support@veloxo.in.
