Meet Velo · SI coach

A Sports Intelligence coach that knows your plan and your rides.

Ask why today is easy, how hard to push, what to eat on a long ride, or how to pace your race. Velo answers from your own training, not generic tips.

I'm tired today. Should I still do intervals?
You rode 2h 40m on Sunday, more than planned. Swap today for an easy 45 min spin and move the intervals to Thursday. You'll get more out of them.
What should I eat for the 80 km?
Something every 20 minutes: a banana or a bar, and a bottle an hour. Start before you feel hungry.
How it works

From goal to finish line in three steps.

  1. Tell Velo your goal

    Your level, your riding days and what you're training for: an event, getting fitter, or your own target.

  2. Ride the workouts

    They show up on your Wahoo, in Zwift or on your Garmin, with clear targets. No device? Follow them in the app.

  3. Sync, review, improve

    Rides come back on their own. Velo reviews your week and keeps your plan on track for race day.

Questions

Is Veloxo free?

Yes, while we test. Velo Pro (more coaching) and Organiser Pro (more events a year) come later, and you'll always be told before anything costs money.

Do I need a bike computer?

No. You can follow every workout in the app and mark it done. With a Wahoo, Zwift or a Garmin (through Intervals.icu), workouts go to your device and rides sync back on their own.

Does Veloxo record my rides?

No. Your device or Zwift records the ride. Veloxo plans, coaches and reads the finished ride back.

Where can I use Veloxo?

Training works anywhere. Events come from the cycling clubs on Veloxo, and new clubs are joining while we test. Run a club? List your club.

How do I get the app?

The Android app is in testing. Join the beta and we'll send it to you, or email veloxo.si@gmail.com. iPhone comes later.
