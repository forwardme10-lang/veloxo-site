How it works

From goal to finish line in three steps.

01

Tell Velo your goal. Your level, your riding days and what you’re training for: an event, getting fitter, or your own target.

02

Ride the workouts. They show up on your Wahoo, in Zwift or on your Garmin, with clear targets. No device? Follow them in the app.

03

Sync, review, improve. Finished rides sync automatically. Velo reviews your week and keeps you on track for race day.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Veloxo free?

Yes, while we test. Velo Pro (more coaching) and Organiser Pro (more events a year) come later, and you’ll always be told before anything costs money.

Do I need a bike computer?

No. Workouts go straight to a Wahoo, and to Zwift or Garmin through Intervals.icu. Without a device, you can follow every workout in the app.

Does Veloxo record my rides?

No. Your device or Zwift records the ride, then it syncs to Veloxo automatically and ticks off your plan.

Where can I use Veloxo?

Training works anywhere. Events come from the cycling clubs that list them on Veloxo, and more clubs join as we grow.

How do I get the app?

Veloxo is in beta on Android. Join the beta and we’ll send you the app. More answers on the Support page.
