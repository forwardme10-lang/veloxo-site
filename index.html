I’m tired today. Should I still do intervals?

VELOYou rode 2h 40m on Sunday, more than planned. Swap today for an easy 45 min spin and move the intervals to Thursday. You’ll get more out of them.

What should I eat for the 80 km?

VELOSomething every 20 minutes: a banana or a bar, and a bottle an hour. Start before you feel hungry.

How it works

From goal to finish line in three steps.

01

Tell Velo your goal. Your level, your riding days and what you’re training for: an event, getting fitter, or your own target.

02

Ride the workouts. They show up on your Wahoo, in Zwift or on your Garmin, with clear targets. No device? Follow them in the app.

03

Sync, review, improve. Rides come back on their own. Velo reviews your week and keeps your plan on track for race day.

For cycling clubs

Run your events without spreadsheets.

Publish, collect entry fees, check riders in and publish results from your phone.

  • 01

    Publish in minutes

    Distances, slots and fees, early bird, race-day schedule, route support and rules.

  • 02

    Get paid directly

    Riders pay your club’s UPI ID. You confirm each payment and they get a bib. Veloxo never holds rider money.

  • 03

    Race day and results

    Attendance by bib, finish times, results and certificates, plus updates pushed to riders.

  • 04

    Bring your team

    Invite volunteers for attendance and results. Payments stay with the club owner.

FAQ

Questions

Is Veloxo free?

Yes, while we test. Velo Pro (more coaching) and Organiser Pro (more events a year) come later, and you’ll always be told before anything costs money.

Do I need a bike computer?

No. Workouts go to your Wahoo, or to Zwift and Garmin through Intervals.icu. No device? Follow them in the app.

Does Veloxo record my rides?

Your device or Zwift records the ride. Finished rides come back to Veloxo on their own and tick off your plan.

Is it only for Chennai?

For now. Training works anywhere, but the events are Chennai clubs while we test. More cities will follow.

How do I get the app?

Veloxo is in beta on Android. Join the beta and we’ll send you the app. More answers on the Support page.
